SMARTTRADEX LOGISTICS AGREEMENT - CONTRACT #SHP-GLOBAL-2025

SECTION 4: QUALITY CONTROL & PERISHABLES

4.1 TEMPERATURE CONTROLS
For sensitive pharmaceutical shipments (Class A), the internal container temperature must be maintained between 2°C and 8°C.
Any deviation below 0°C or above 10°C for a duration longer than 5 minutes constitutes a "Critical Breach".

4.2 PENALTIES
In the event of a Critical Breach, the Buyer reserves the right to reject the shipment immediately.
A penalty fee of 15% of the invoice value will be automatically deducted from the escrow.

4.3 SHOCK & VIBRATION
Maximum allowable shock is 2.0G. Deviations above this indicate mishandling.
